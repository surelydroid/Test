Advantages of Sekisui Modular

Enhancing the build process with factory precision, speed, and exceptional quality.

Accelerated Timelines

Factory-based construction minimizes weather delays and allows simultaneous site work, significantly reducing time to occupancy.

By shifting up to 80% of construction activity to our climate-controlled facility, we eliminate seasonal delays. Site preparation and foundation work happen concurrently with module fabrication, ensuring a seamless, rapid deployment to the site.

Uncompromising Quality

Controlled indoor environments ensure precision assembly, leading to superior material integration and structural integrity.

Our CSA-certified processes include rigorous quality checks at every station. Using advanced Japanese manufacturing principles, we achieve millimeter precision that is virtually impossible to replicate on a traditional job site.

Sustainable & Efficient

Reduced site waste and high thermal performance contribute to greener buildings and lower long-term operating costs.

Modular construction reduces material waste by up to 40%. Furthermore, the extreme airtightness and superior insulation of our modules result in remarkable energy efficiency, easily meeting advanced step-code requirements across Canada.

Our Heritage

A Legacy of Innovation

From the first plastic injection molding in Japan to the future of Canadian housing.

1947

The Foundation

Sekisui Chemical Co., Ltd. is established in Osaka, Japan, pioneering the comprehensive commercialization of plastics in the post-war era.

1970

Birth of Sekisui Heim

Exhibited the "Heim M1" at the International Good Living Show—the first modular house built with over 80% production efficiency.

1997

Environmental Leadership

Commenced full-scale sales of modular housing equipped with solar-power generation systems, leading the industry in sustainable residential design.

2009

International Expansion

About Sekisui Canada Modular Solutions

Our Global Heritage, Local Commitment

Sekisui Canada Modular Solutions Inc. leverages the decades-long global expertise of Sekisui Chemical Co., Ltd. in innovative building technologies and sustainable manufacturing. We bring this world-class pedigree directly to the Canadian market, adapting proven systems to meet the unique challenges and regulatory environment of every province. Our mission is to transform the construction industry through off-site fabrication, delivering superior, predictable, and environmentally responsible projects.

We are committed to building long-term partnerships with Canadian developers, architects, and institutions, providing a smarter path to occupancy for everything from multi-unit residential buildings to specialized commercial facilities.
